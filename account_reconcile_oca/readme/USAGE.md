## Bank reconcile

Access Invoicing / Dashboard with a user with Full Acounting
capabilities. Select reconcile on the journal of your choice.

## Early payment discount

Early payment discount configured on payment terms is handled automatically in
the reconciliation widget. No dedicated reconcile model per payment term is
required.

When the bank statement amount matches the discounted amount and the statement
date is within the discount date, invoice lines are fully reconciled and the
required discount/tax counterpart lines are generated automatically.

## Account reconcile

Access Invoicing / Accounting / Actions / Reconcile All the possible
reconcile options will show and you will be able to reconcile properly.
You can access the same widget from accounts and Partners.
