## Bank reconcile

Access Invoicing / Dashboard with a user with Full Acounting
capabilities. Select reconcile on the journal of your choice.

## Account reconcile

Access Invoicing / Accounting / Actions / Reconcile All the possible
reconcile options will show and you will be able to reconcile properly.
You can access the same widget from accounts and Partners.

## Early payment discounts

Early payment discounts (cash discounts) defined on payment terms are applied
automatically:

- When an invoice matching model matches the statement line with the discounted
  amount of invoices, before their discount date.
- When you manually add the journal item of an invoice and the amount left on the
  statement line is exactly its discounted amount, before its discount date.

The invoices are then fully paid and the discount lines (discount and tax reduction,
depending on the payment term configuration) are generated on the early payment
discount accounts of the company, as done by the Register Payment wizard. These
accounts must be set in Invoicing / Configuration / Settings / Default Accounts.

The discount only applies to the first payment of an invoice: a partial payment
never gets a prorated discount.
