1.  Go to *Invoicing \> Review \> Control \> Journal Items*.
2.  Select the journal items to reconcile. They must share the same reconcilable account,
    not be fully reconciled yet, and include at least one debit and one credit.
3.  Click **Reconcile** in the list header. The wizard shows the account, the number of
    journal items and their total debit and credit, with a warning when they belong to
    several partners.
4.  If the journal items are balanced, click **Full Reconcile**.
5.  Otherwise, click **Partial Reconcile** to leave the difference open, or
    **Reconcile with Write-off** to settle it:
    1.  Select a **Model**, or fill in the **Journal**, **Date**, **Reference**,
        **Write-off Account** and **Analytic** yourself.
    2.  Click **Reconcile with Write-off**: a write-off journal entry is posted for the
        difference and all the journal items are fully reconciled.
